Protein-Ligand Interaction Explorer

Grew out of my IEEE paper on computational PROTAC biology. I wanted the method to be something other students could actually use.

What it does
Visualizes how a small molecule sits in a protein's binding site and uses machine learning to predict binding strength, so researchers can screen candidates before going to the lab.

PauseCheck

Inspired by my CNA work. Residents I cared for talked about calls and texts from people pretending to be their bank or their grandkids.

What it does
A senior shares a suspicious text, link or call and gets a plain-language answer, the warning signs named, and one clear next step. Phase 2, now in development, asks them to pause on high-risk messages and alerts a trusted family member.

Designer and Developer
